92 consumers trust Influencer Recommendations 

Recent data from http://Market.us's Creator Economy Market report (2026) shows how far affiliate marketing has come:
 
→ The global creator economy is valued at $149.4B in 2024 and projected to hit $1,072.8B by 2034 (21.8% CAGR)

→ Affiliate marketing's share of creator monetization has grown from 12.2% (2019) to 12.5% (2024)

→ Affiliate and live-shopping commissions now typically run 5–10% of product value

→ Creators using integrated storefronts see earnings jump by ~18%

→ 92% of consumers say they trust influencer recommendations over traditional advertising
 
Affiliate marketing isn't a standalone tactic anymore, it's built into checkout flows, live shopping, and creator storefronts. The commission model has become infrastructure, not an afterthought.
