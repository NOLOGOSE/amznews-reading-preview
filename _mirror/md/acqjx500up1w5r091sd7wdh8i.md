## 正文

New! Amazon is pushing a smarter way to reduce returns.

Amazon's Replacement Parts Program lets sellers replace broken or missing components instead of accepting a full product return.

Amazon reports that 64% of customers who received replacement parts in 2025 kept their products.

Fewer returns, lower costs, happier customers. A potential game changer.
