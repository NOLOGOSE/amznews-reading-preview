## 摘要

在理解 Listing 优化之前，你不能将其外包。你需要具备足够的知识来辨别工作的好坏。

我的分工如下：离岸团队负责 SEO，内部设计师负责图片，广告机构负责投放广告。

先学会它，然后再移交出去。

## 正文

You can't outsource listing optimization before you understand it. You need to know enough to tell good work from bad.

Here's how mine is split: an offshore team handles SEO, a designer in-house does the images, and an ad agency runs the ads.

Learn it first, then hand it off.
