14 wholesale accounts in 45 days.

Jett has done retail arbitrage for 2.5 years, around $60K a month. He watched gating get stricter and wanted something that lasts.

He spent 6 months trying brand direct on his own and landed a couple of brands.

His business was already set up, so we skipped straight to which brands to target and how to pitch them.

Selling on Amazon and opening brand accounts are two different skills.
