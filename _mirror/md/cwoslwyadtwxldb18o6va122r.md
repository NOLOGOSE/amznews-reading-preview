## 摘要

原文作者 SaulSellsStuff 自述其参与 FlipAlert 工具，称11天内报销（reimbursed）20万美元、近7500名用户试用；原文另引其此前推文称正接近400万美元报销额，并称无额外费用。原文称近一个月改进选品与报告模块，在 flips 板块将自有品牌商品减少90%，并支持清晰的损益表和补货报告。团队称正在测试移动端 App 和 Chrome 扩展，计划推出由 FA 亚马逊模块与 @Lemmebuyit_ 共同支持的线索追踪功能，以获取数百万商品的实时零售价，并在 Q4 前推出两个新的亚马逊直采系统。上述金额、用户数与效果均为作者自述，本站尚未独立核验。原文来自该工具方，存在相关商业利益，并非独立测评。

## 正文

$200,000 reimbursed in 11 days. 

Passed our latest milestone, almost 7,500 of you have given @FlipAlert a shot and that just is a bit unreal. 

Quite a few improvements over the last month on how our sourcing and reporting modules work. 

From cutting down 90% of private label items in our flips section to enabling clear P&Ls and replenishment reports. 

What’s next? 

We’ve been beta testing the FA mobile app and chrome extension to release soon. Both built over the last 13 months on features we’ve always wanted. More to come there. 

New modules? 

Lead tracking backed by both FAs amazon modules and @Lemmebuyit_ for up to date retail pricing on millions of items you’re monitoring so you know exactly when to purchase or replen. 

Two new sourcing systems focused on Amazon directly ahead of Q4.  

On a personal level I’ve been spending time away from screens more in prep for November. I appreciate everyone who keeps giving us a shot at helping your business grow or thrive. 

Wouldn’t be able to do this without the folks who use FA every day.
