## 摘要

我们仓库收到的一些货物完全没有标签。空瓶子，直接来自制造商。

这仅占我们采购量的极小比例，但确实会发生，且这些货物都无法发往亚马逊。

在发往 FBA 前务必检查每一批入库货物，并在 30 天内向供应商标记此问题。

## 正文

Some of what shows up at our warehouse has no label at all. Blank bottles, straight from the manufacturer.

It's a fraction of a percent of what we buy, but it happens, and none of it can go to Amazon.

Check every inbound before it ships to FBA, and flag it to the supplier within 30 days.
