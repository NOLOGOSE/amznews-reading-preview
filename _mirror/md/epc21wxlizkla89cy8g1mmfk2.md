## 摘要

现在在亚马逊搜索几乎任何内容，第一行全是广告。在第一个自然结果出现之前有 5 个赞助位。

大多数购物者不会翻过第一页的前半部分。

未投放 PPC 的品牌在大多数搜索中起始位置都在首屏以下。

## 正文

Search almost anything on Amazon right now and the entire first row is ads. 5 sponsored spots before the first organic result.

Most shoppers never scroll past the first half of page one.

A brand that isn't running PPC is starting most searches below the fold.
