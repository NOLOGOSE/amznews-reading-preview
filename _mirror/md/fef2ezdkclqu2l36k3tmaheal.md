## 摘要

亚马逊宣布自2026年12月18日起，大多数美国FBM订单退货需卖家提供预付退货标签。卖家须在7天内上传标签或全额退款，否则亚马逊可能自动执行无退货退款并从账户扣款，重型和大件商品暂时豁免。作者转发此消息并评论称这是卖家应分散亚马逊依赖的又一理由。

> FYI for FBM sellers: Amazon says that starting December 18, 2026, most US returns for seller-fulfilled orders will require a prepaid return label.
> 
> Within seven calendar days of a return request, sellers must upload the label through Manage Seller-Fulfilled Returns or issue a full refund without requiring the item back. If neither happens, Amazon says it may automatically issue a returnless refund and deduct the amount from the seller’s account.
> 
> Heavy and bulky items are temporarily excluded. Labels sent outside Amazon’s returns workflow won’t count.
