## 摘要

一旦品牌在商品详情页上传了自己的视频，亚马逊达人就可以添加他们的视频。

他们根据品类不同，每笔销售可获得约 1-5% 的佣金，因此有动力让产品看起来更好。

大多数品牌尚未上传这第一个视频，这意味着其他人也无法上传。

## 正文

Once a brand posts its own video on a listing, Amazon influencers can add theirs.

They earn about 1-5% commission on every sale depending on the category, so they're motivated to make the product look good.

Most brands haven't posted that first video, which means nobody else can either.
