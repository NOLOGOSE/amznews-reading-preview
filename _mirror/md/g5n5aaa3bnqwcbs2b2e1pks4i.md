## 摘要

实体店售价 $19.99。亚马逊售价 $16.99。TikTok 售价 $25.99。

当品牌定价参差不齐时，客户会感到困惑，品牌也会显得廉价。

在首次通话中指出这一点。这能向品牌表明你在为他们的业务着想。

## 正文

$19.99 in stores. $16.99 on Amazon. $25.99 on TikTok.

When a brand's pricing is all over the place, customers get confused and the brand starts to look cheap.

Point that out on your first call. It shows the brand you're thinking about their business.
