## 摘要

如果 CTR 疲软，CVR 疲软，定价不当且评论落后……调整出价并不能凭空创造需求。

## 正文

One of the harder things to tell an Amazon seller:

Your PPC might not be the problem.

If CTR is weak, CVR is weak, price is off and reviews are behind… changing bids around isn’t going to magically create demand.

PPC amplifies what’s already there.
