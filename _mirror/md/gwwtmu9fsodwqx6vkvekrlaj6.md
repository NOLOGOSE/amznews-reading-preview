Missed out on our Engage with Amazon event with the Customer Service by Amazon (CSBA) team? Here's the recap: ⬇️
The team shared key updates: fewer returnless refunds, automatic reimbursements when using Buy Shipping or Veeqo, no more 95% Valid Tracking Rate requirement, all CSBA alerts now in Buyer-Seller Messages, and smarter insights with daily CPU monitoring.
