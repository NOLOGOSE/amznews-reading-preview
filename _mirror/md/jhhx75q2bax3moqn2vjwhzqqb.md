## 摘要

有时亚马逊销量下滑并非 PPC 问题。

产品已不再处于生命周期的同一阶段。

更多竞争对手
更优的报价
搜索趋势改变
产品变得陈旧

你可以永远优化广告，却仍在解决错误的问题。

## 正文

Sometimes declining Amazon sales aren’t a PPC problem.

The product just isn’t in the same part of its lifecycle anymore.

more competitors
better offers
search trends changed
product got stale

You can optimize ads forever and still be fighting the wrong problem.
