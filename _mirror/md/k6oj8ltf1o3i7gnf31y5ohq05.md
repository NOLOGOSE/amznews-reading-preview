## 摘要

别再开口就问“能开批发账户吗？”

找个小品牌，免费帮他们优化一个商品详情页。你练了手，如果销量涨了，你就有了案例。

这样开口就容易多了：把这个链接的独家给我，如果销量继续涨，我们再谈整个目录的合作。

## 正文

Stop opening with "Can we open a wholesale account?"

Find a small brand and optimize one of their listings for free. You learn the skill, and if their sales go up, you've got a case study.

Then the ask gets easy: give me the exclusive on this listing, and if sales keep moving, we talk about the rest of the catalog.
