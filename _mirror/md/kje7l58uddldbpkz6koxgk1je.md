## 摘要

一个例子说明，作为亚马逊对亚马逊卖家，你需要同时关注大众定价和企业定价。

个人账户 ROI 为 4%
企业账户 ROI 为 65%

同一产品，仅因登录身份不同，价格降幅就不同。

## 正文

An example of why as an Amazon to Amazon seller you’ll want to be aware of both the general public pricing and business pricing. 

4% ROI on personal accounts 
65% ROI on business accounts 

Same product just different price drops depending on how you’re signed in.
