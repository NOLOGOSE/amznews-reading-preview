## 摘要

许多亚马逊新手卖家因害怕在采购库存时出错，而不敢看 Keepa 图表。

如果你花 $10 买一个测试单元，最终亏损 $1 退出该商品详情页，你仍能收回 $9 的营运资金。即使购买 50 个测试单元，这也只是实体亚马逊运营中 $50 的学费。

## 正文

A lot of new Amazon sellers freeze on Keepa charts because they are terrified of being wrong on an inventory buy.

If you put $10 into a test unit and end up taking a small $1 loss to exit the listing, you still get $9 of your working capital back. Even across 50 test units, that is a $50 education in physical Amazon operations.

The price of admission to learn this business is remarkably cheap. Small test buys build the confidence that endless spreadsheet analysis never will.
