## 正文

Arbitrage sucks. Wholesale sucks. You hear it all. I did both and here is my honest take.

Arbitrage wins on two things. You can start with a few hundred dollars, and the margins are higher. My wholesale average is about 15%.

Wholesale wins on risk, scale, how long it lasts, and whether you can ever sell the business.
