## 摘要

唤醒品牌最快的方式，是把他们的商品详情页放在竞争对手旁边。

竞争对手有对比图表、生活场景图、A+ Content 和视频。而他们什么都没有。

这种对比替你说明了问题。

## 正文

The fastest way to wake a brand up is to put their listing next to a competitor's.

The competitor has comparison charts, lifestyle photos, A+ content, and a video. They have none of it.

The comparison makes the case for you.
