## 摘要

亚马逊的建议出价并非告诉你盈利的出价。

它基本上是在告诉你拍卖市场要求的出价。

这两者截然不同。

你的出价应首先基于你的经济状况，而不是那个小小的推荐框。

## 正文

Amazon’s suggested bid is not Amazon telling you the profitable bid.

It’s basically telling you what the auction is asking for.

Those are very different things.

Your bid should come from your economics first. Not the little recommendation box.
