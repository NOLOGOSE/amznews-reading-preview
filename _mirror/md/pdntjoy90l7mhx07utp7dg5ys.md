## 摘要

两家亚马逊业务。营收相同，利润相同。

一家是套利。一家是批发。

只有一家能被出售。套利业务是一系列交易，一旦品牌清理其商品详情页就会终止。批发业务是一个账户组合，会持续复购。

如果你打算长期经营，请选择那个有退出路径的模式。

## 正文

Two Amazon businesses. Same revenue, same profit.

One is arbitrage. One is wholesale.

Only one of them can ever be sold. An arbitrage business is a set of deals that close the moment a brand cleans up its listings. A wholesale business is a portfolio of accounts that keep reordering.

If you're building something for years, build the one with an exit.
