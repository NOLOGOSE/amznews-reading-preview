Saul分享通过Urban Outfitters等实体店清仓区采购黑胶唱片在亚马逊转售的经验，实现$5-10成本转化为$35-$60售价。他建议探索CVS、Walgreens等非传统门店及商场角落以发现库存机会，指出常规商店仍有大量优质货源可供挖掘。

## 正文

In my series to help resellers think outside of the box. 

At one point Urban Outfitters was my number one source of Vinyl Records to sell on Amazon. 

Physically locations had a section and dozens would end up on sale / clearance. 

$5-10 into $35-$60 

You can source and do very well at conventional stores. You can also find lots of great inventory at stores you may not be thinking about. 

Worth a stop in at different stores at your local mall to get a better sense of what they sell. Then do the same at strip malls, etc. 

One Black Friday years ago I only sourced at CVS / Walgreens for toys 🤷‍♂️
