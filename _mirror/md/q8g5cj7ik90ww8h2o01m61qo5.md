## 摘要

我品牌分析中的一个品牌：上月约 49,000 次加入购物车。约 9,200 次购买。

大约每 5 个将其加入购物车的人中，有 1 人实际购买了它。

品牌分析展示了该漏斗的每一步，从搜索到购买。找出人们流失的地方，那就是你需要修复的商品详情页部分。

## 正文

One brand in my Brand Analytics: about 49,000 add to carts last month. About 9,200 purchases.

Roughly 1 in 5 people who put it in their cart actually bought it.

Brand Analytics shows every step of that funnel, from search to purchase. Find where people drop off and that's the part of the listing you fix.
