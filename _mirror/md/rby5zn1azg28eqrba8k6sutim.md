## 摘要

在采购任何高利润的亚马逊补货商品前，请在 Keepa 上查看历史卖家数量。

当报价中的卖家数量在一夜之间从 12 家骤降至 1 或 2 家时，这通常意味着品牌执法行动或知识产权投诉。额外的利润率永远不值得以账户健康受损为代价。

一张显示竞争稳定的干净图表，比追逐高风险的商品详情页更能保护你的资金。

## 正文

Before buying any Amazon replen with great margins, check the historical seller count on Keepa.

When an offer count drops from 12 sellers down to 1 or 2 overnight, that usually signals a brand enforcement sweep or an IP complaint. The extra profit margin is never worth an Account Health strike.

A clean chart with stable competition protects your capital far better than chasing a risky listing.
