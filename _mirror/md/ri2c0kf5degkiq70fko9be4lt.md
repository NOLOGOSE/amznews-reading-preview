## 摘要

ASGTG社区分享一卖家案例，该卖家声称拥有并制造自有品牌超10年且已备案Brand Registry，却收到亚马逊通知将于10月28日移除其Listing，理由是品牌不再符合第三方卖家资格。亚马逊表示具有Brand Representative角色的卖家可继续销售，但此规则让实际品牌所有者感到困惑，发帖人询问是否有其他类似情况以评估影响范围。

## 正文

This is a strange one. An @amazon  seller says they’ve owned and manufactured their own brand for more than 10 years, are enrolled in Brand Registry, and never sell third-party products. 

Yet Amazon sent them this notice saying their listings will be removed October 28 because the brand is no longer eligible for third-party sellers. 

Amazon says sellers with the Brand Representative role can continue selling, which makes this even more confusing for actual brand owners receiving the notice.

Has anyone else received this despite owning the brand and having the correct Brand Registry roles?

Trying to understand how widespread this is.
