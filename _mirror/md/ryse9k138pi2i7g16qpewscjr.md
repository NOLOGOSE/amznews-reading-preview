## 摘要

亚马逊新卖家常犯的一个错误是，发现好产品后兴奋不已，在未测试实际售罄率前就大量进货。

应采取“浅尝辄止、广泛撒网”的策略。首批发货建议总共约50件，分散在不同商品上。例如10个ASIN各发5件，或25个ASIN各发2件。

如果某个链接因价格战失败或被重新分类导致费用增加，你的资金不会全部损失，同时也能了解产品在现实中的周转情况。

## 正文

A mistake new Amazon sellers make is they get excited about finding a good item and buy way too much of it before testing their actual sell-through rate.

Go an inch deep and a mile wide instead. On your first shipment, aim for roughly 50 units total spread across different items. That could be 5 units of 10 ASINs, or 2 units of 25 ASINs.

If a listing tanks on price or gets reclassified with higher fees, your capital is not wiped out, and you learn how products turn in real life.
