## 摘要

某品牌向我们的一位学员提供独家合作机会。条件是：先清理其过剩库存以证明实力。

我也曾处于这种境地。过剩库存之所以是过剩，必有原因。有时问题出在商品详情页（Listing），有时则是没人想要该产品。

选定一个 SKU，修复 Listing，并提前设定预期。没人能把滞销品变成畅销品。

## 正文

A brand offered one of our students an exclusive. The catch: liquidate their surplus inventory first to prove himself.

I've been in that spot. Surplus is surplus for a reason. Sometimes the listing is the problem. Sometimes nobody wants the product.

Take one SKU, fix the listing, and set expectations up front. Nobody turns a dead product into a bestseller.
