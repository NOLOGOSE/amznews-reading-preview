## 摘要

据X用户@guyfosel转述@wallstengine的内容，YouTube已将亚马逊纳入其购物联盟计划，符合条件的美国创作者可在Shorts、长视频和直播中直接标记亚马逊产品。该转述称，集成支持自动产品打标，并在YouTube Studio内追踪点击和收益，佣金通过AdSense发放。转述还称YouTube表示7月测试发现产品标签比仅靠描述链接多带来110%以上的点击量；该测试数据来自转述，本站尚未独立核验。

> YOUTUBE BRINGS AMAZON DIRECTLY INTO ITS SHOPPING AFFILIATE PROGRAM
> 
> Eligible U.S. creators can now link their Amazon Associates or Influencer account to YouTube and tag Amazon products directly inside Shorts, long-form videos and livestreams, instead of relying on links buried in descriptions.
> 
> The integration pulls from a curated Amazon catalog, supports automatic product tagging, tracks clicks and earnings inside YouTube Studio, and pays Amazon commissions through AdSense alongside creators’ existing YouTube affiliate earnings.
> 
> For viewers, the tags send them directly to Amazon to complete the purchase. YouTube says a July test found product tags generated 110%+ more clicks than description links alone.
