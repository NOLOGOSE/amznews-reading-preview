## 摘要

作者 SaulSellsStuff 在 X 上分享个人在 Prime 促销期间的采购经历：Prime 独家优惠无法用于 A2A，但许多商品在 Walmart 等零售商处有价格匹配；作者主要购买消耗品和玩具，并称自己购买了超过 1,000 包 Pur 口香糖，12 包装从 $35+ 降至 $10。作者还提到部分做 TikTok 直播的食品卖家加入了 FlipAlert，并称他们抓住了类似交易。以上均为作者个人自述，本站尚未独立核验。

## 正文

Folks starting to share how they did. 

The prime only deals were great, sadly we couldn’t buy them for A2A. However a ton were matching or matched across retailers like Walmart. 

This sale was mainly consumables and toys for me. Mix of items going back up now and short holds for December pricing. 

For example I purchased over 1,000 packs of Pur gum. Amazon happened to be matching Walmart across various bundles. 

So a 12 pack became $10 down from $35+ 

Recently some food sellers that do TikTok live have joined us at FlipAlert and I know they grabbed those kind of deals as well. 

Surge of new markets it’s become more interesting to look at deals across multiple platforms. 

Not everyone is going to spend tens of thousands but it isn’t for a lack of opportunity. 

I’m still thrilled when I snag 3 of something and can make $60 🥹
