## 摘要

我顺着“$10-$15 价格区间降低佣金”这个想法延伸一下，更广泛地建议：对平台上年营收低于 $1m 的品牌降低佣金。

将首笔 $1m 营收的 15% 佣金降至 7.5%。

这是品牌生命周期中最艰难的阶段，许多有潜力的品牌/产品在此期间因亚马逊费用负担过重而窒息。

## 正文

I’ll riff on this idea of a lower referral fee for the $10-$15 price tier and suggest, more broadly, a lower referral fee for brands doing less than $1m/yr on the platform.

Bring the 15% referral fee down to 7.5% for the first million in revenue.

This is the hardest stage in a brand’s lifecycle and many viable brands/products suffocate under the weight of Amazon fees during this period.
