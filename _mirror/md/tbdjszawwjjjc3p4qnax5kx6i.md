## 摘要

优惠券的作用不止于降价。它会在搜索结果页添加一个徽章，紧邻那些没有该徽章的商品详情页。

“订阅并节省”功能也是如此。“订阅额外享受 15% 折扣”在用户点击前就会显示出来。

这些徽章是你拱手让给竞争对手的点击量。

## 正文

A coupon does more than lower the price. It puts a badge on the search results page, right next to listings that don't have one.

Subscribe and save does the same thing. "Extra 15% off when you subscribe" shows up before anyone even clicks.

Badges are clicks you're giving away to competitors.
