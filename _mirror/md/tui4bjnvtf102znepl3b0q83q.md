## 摘要

当商品详情页发生变动时，大多数卖家会自行应对。他们会降价或停止购买。

先给品牌方发邮件。告诉他们你观察到的情况，并询问他们在亚马逊上的计划。

关键在于沟通，这也是你提出独家合作的最佳契机。

## 正文

When something changes on a listing, most sellers react on their own. They drop the price or stop buying.

Email the brand first. Tell them what you're seeing and ask what their plan is for Amazon.

It's always about communication, and it's your best opening to bring up an exclusive.
