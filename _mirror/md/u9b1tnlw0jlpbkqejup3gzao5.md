## 摘要

如果你的亚马逊业务需要每个周末在零售货架扫描条形码，那你只是给自己建了个周末轮班。

拿着手机扫码器跑遍 Target 或 Walmart 能帮你积累最初几千美元的营运资金。真正的运营规模来自线上采购、自动化的 Keepa 筛选以及无论你是否进店都能运行的补货系统。

能够离开商店货架而销售不中断，这才是重点。

## 正文

If your Amazon business requires scanning barcodes in retail aisles every weekend, you built yourself a weekend shift.

Running through Target or Walmart with a phone scanner works to build your first few thousand dollars of working capital. Real operational scale comes from online sourcing, automated Keepa filters, and replen systems that run whether you step foot in a store or not.

Being able to step away from store aisles without sales stopping is the whole point.
