## 摘要

Listing 优化归结为一个销售漏斗：

1. 被看见：SEO 和 PPC
2. 被点击：主图、价格、评论
3. 被购买：内容、A+、视频

找出漏损的环节，优先修复它。

## 正文

Listing optimization comes down to a sales funnel:

1. Getting seen: SEO and PPC
2. Getting clicked: main image, price, reviews
3. Getting bought: content, A+, video

Find the stage that's leaking and fix that one first.
