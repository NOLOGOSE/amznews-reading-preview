## 正文

I don’t and can’t condone using multiple amazon accounts to get around quantity limits, it’s just unnecessary risk on your seller account. 

But man there are some deals today I’d buy 10,000 of in a heartbeat and all I could get was 300-400. 

Day two of prime day is showing up a bit more than day one for me personally. 

Still seeing over $500,000 in spend in one day from the @FlipAlert folks is 👑

Lots of Amazon to Walmart happening this year, more than I recall on previous sales.
