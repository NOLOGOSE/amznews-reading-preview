## 摘要

亚马逊推出新的多渠道工具，旨在将 eBay、Shopify、TikTok 和 Walmart 的商品详情页、订单及利润数据整合至 Seller Central。作者指出该功能虽提供便利，但质疑卖家是否愿意向亚马逊开放其他销售渠道的数据。

## 正文

Amazon’s new multichannel tools promise to bring eBay, Shopify, TikTok and Walmart into Seller Central, with listings, orders and profitability all in one place.

The convenience sounds appealing. But I wonder how many sellers will want Amazon to see what’s happening across their other channels. Will sellers be comfortable connecting all that data to Amazon?

Would you use it?
