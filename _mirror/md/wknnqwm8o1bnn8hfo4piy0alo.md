## 摘要

亚马逊FBM卖家近期频繁遭遇发货后运费补扣，部分标签费用从24美元涨至50美元。今年通过Seller Central购买的运费标签均加收7%费用，该现象自8月持续至今。卖家可在财务交易页搜索“Chargeback due to under charging postage”核实具体扣款记录。

## 正文

For my Amazon FBM Sellers:

In the last 60 days we have seen a massive uptick in Amazon adding shipping charges after shipment. 

Some of these items we have sold before paying $24 a label are now being upcharged to $50 a label. 

Started in August and continues into October. 

All shipping purchased through seller central, is adding 7% to the total label costs this year. 

Opening tickets now, but to check this yourself:

https://sellercentral.amazon.com/manage/finance/transactions

Then look for "Chargeback due to under charging postage label purchased"

You can also grab your entire year report and send it to your LLM of choice to see the full damage.
