## 摘要

如果你的亚马逊业务需要每个周末在零售货架上扫描条形码，那你就是给自己排了个周末班。

拿着手机扫描仪跑遍 Target 或 Walmart 确实能建立不错的利润空间，但真正的运营规模来自于线上采购、自动化的 Keepa 筛选器以及无论你是否进店都能运行的补货系统。

能够离开商店货架而销售不中断，这才是重点所在。

## 正文

If your Amazon business requires scanning barcodes in retail aisles every weekend, you built yourself a weekend shift.

Running through Target or Walmart with a phone scanner works to build some great profit margins, but real operational scale comes from online sourcing, automated Keepa filters, and replen systems that run whether you step foot in a store or not.

Being able to step away from store aisles without sales stopping is the whole point.
