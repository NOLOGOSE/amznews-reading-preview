## 摘要

不在亚马逊上的品牌并不自动意味着机会。

如果是大品牌，或在 Shopify、TikTok 上获得真实增长，可以尝试合作。否则需谨慎。并非所有产品都适合在亚马逊销售。

在通过该渠道销售品牌前，请先核实需求。

## 正文

A brand that isn't on Amazon isn't automatically an opportunity.

If it's a big brand, or it's getting real traction on Shopify or TikTok, pitch them. If not, be careful. Not every product works on Amazon.

Check the demand before you sell a brand on the channel.
