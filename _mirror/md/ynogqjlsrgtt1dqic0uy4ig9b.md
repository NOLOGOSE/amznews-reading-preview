## 摘要

如果你获得品牌备案权限，请使用 Brand Analytics。

它展示整个转化漏斗：搜索、曝光、页面浏览量、加入购物车、购买。下载报告，通过 AI 分析，你就能准确看到哪个环节正在流失销量。

这些数据是你向品牌方展示你辛勤工作的筹码。

## 正文

If you get Brand Registry access, use Brand Analytics.

It shows the whole funnel: searches, impressions, page views, add to carts, purchases. Download the reports, run them through AI, and you'll see exactly which stage is losing sales.

That data is your leverage to show the brand your hard work.
