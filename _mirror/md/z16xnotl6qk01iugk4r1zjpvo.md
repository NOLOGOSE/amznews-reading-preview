## 摘要

大多数卖家为店铺运行自动评论请求工具。你也可以为产品运行一个。

它不会在一夜之间让评论翻倍，但会增加评论产生的频率，而评论数量是点击率的主要驱动因素之一。

如果你只运行了一个，本周请设置另一个。

## 正文

Most sellers run an automated feedback requester for their storefront. You can run one for your products too.

It won't double your reviews overnight, but it bumps how often reviews come in, and review count is one of the biggest drivers of clicks.

If you're only running one, set up the other this week.
