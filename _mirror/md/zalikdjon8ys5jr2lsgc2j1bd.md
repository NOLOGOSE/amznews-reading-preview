## 摘要

Rambler_Amz在X上评论51Degrees自报调查：该机构分析截至2026年5月29日的一年内30亿次网站访问，称AmazonBot与Meta-External-Agent合计占其追踪到的AI机器人流量的63%，整体AI机器人流量同比增62.5%，占全部网络会话13%（一年前为8%）；并称出版商仅通过robots.txt屏蔽约21%的已知AI爬虫。上述数据来自该机构自报，本站尚未独立核验，不能推广为亚马逊平台流量或总体结论。作者对合作伙伴亚马逊listing流量是否混有AI与人类表示不确定。

## 正文

AI bots may be reading your listings more than actual shoppers are

51Degrees analyzed 3 billion website visits (year to May 29, 2026)

AmazonBot + Meta-External-Agent = 63% of all AI bot traffic tracked

On May 29 alone: AmazonBot logged 6.43M sessions, Meta's bot 4.23M > combined, more than AhrefsBot + BingBot put together

Overall AI bot traffic: +62.5% YoY, now 13% of all web sessions (up from 8% a year ago)

The catch: these bots scrape the page, feed an AI answer to someone else's question, and the original site never gets the click or the sale.

Publishers are only blocking ~21% of known AI scrapers via robots.txt but most are leaving the door wide open

Not really sure about the traffic numbers we're getting on our partners' listings on Amazon, are they all organic, or a mix of AI and humans? 🤔
