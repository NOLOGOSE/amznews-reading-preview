正文

From storing products in his mom's garage to building a $7 million-a-year business, the seller behind McMiller Games is a reminder of what's possible. When he turned to Fulfillment by Amazon, his mom finally got to "retire" from her daily trips to the post office, and he got to spend more time growing the business.

It's one of countless journeys FBA has helped make possible. This year marks 20 years of FBA, and the service has come a long way: from its very first order back in 2006 to delivering tens of millions of units a day. Storing inventory, picking, packing, shipping, Amazon handles the fulfillment so sellers can focus on what they do best: building.
